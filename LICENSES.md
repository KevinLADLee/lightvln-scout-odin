top-level license overrides the license attached to an individual component.

| `src/drivers/scout_ros2/` | Apache-2.0; see `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, and `UPSTREAM_VERSION` |

The complete standard license texts are available in
[`LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`](LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt) and
[`LICENSES/GPL-3.0-or-later.txt`](LICENSES/GPL-3.0-or-later.txt).

version records that apply to that subset. Confirm ownership and the intended
license before adding new project-owned files.
